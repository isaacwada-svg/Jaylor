# Jaylor Brand Identity Update

## Goal
Replace the current Jaylor identity with the supplied brand guide across the public website and signed-in product, while preserving all existing features and content.

## What will change
- Recreate the supplied mannequin, measuring-tape, needle-and-thread “Jaylor” logo as clean reusable artwork, with full-colour, dark-background, and compact logo versions.
- Replace the current logo everywhere Jaylor appears, including navigation, footers, sign-in and invitation screens, share pages, and the signed-in product.
- Update the app icon, browser icon, and installable-app artwork to match the new logo.
- Replace the current palette with the guide’s navy `#111F39`, gold `#D4AF37`, cream `#F8F4E9`, and charcoal `#1A1A1A`, expressed through the existing design system.
- Use Playfair Display for headings and Montserrat for body and interface text.
- Add “Crafted for you” where a compact brand signature is appropriate, without rewriting product messaging.

## Quality checks
- Preserve existing layouts, links, forms, business logic, and accessibility.
- Keep logo artwork legible at header, mobile, favicon, and app-icon sizes.
- Check the homepage, shared marketing pages, authentication, and signed-in navigation on desktop and mobile.
- Confirm the app builds without errors and no old logo artwork remains in active use.

## Technical details
- Use the uploaded guide as the visual reference; store new website logo assets through the project asset flow.
- Update global semantic colour and typography tokens rather than scattering one-off styles.
- Keep route metadata and SEO content unchanged except for theme colours and icon references.
